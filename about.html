About Lakhani Investment

Trusted with families' savings since 1987. A financial services firm rooted in Rajula, Gujarat, built on relationships, not minimum ticket sizes.

Our story

Close to four decades, one standard.

Lakhani Investment has spent nearly forty years guiding individuals and families through their financial journeys, building investment portfolios, protecting what has been earned, and preparing wealth for the generations that follow.

Through every market cycle and every life stage, we have been a trusted hand at our clients' side. We serve a community of retail investors across the Rajula region and Amreli district, alongside a smaller base of high-net-worth individuals of long standing. Every relationship is treated with the same care, the same attention, and the same uncompromising standard, regardless of the size of the investment.

As a second-generation enterprise, we leverage the rich experience of our founders and the dynamic vision of younger leadership, blending deep industry knowledge with cutting-edge technology.
